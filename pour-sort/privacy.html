Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how Pour Sort ("the game"), made by LowFi Studio ("we", "us"), handles information. In short: the current version of the game does not collect, send or share any personal data.

1. Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not request internet access, so nothing about you or your device leaves your phone.

2. Information stored on your device

To keep your game going, the game saves the following only on your device: your level progress, coins, boosters, owned and selected backgrounds, daily challenge and task progress, and settings (sound, vibration, language, colorblind mode). We cannot see or access this data.

3. Permissions

The game asks for one permission: vibration, for a short buzz when you clear a level. You can turn it off in Settings.

4. Third-party services

The current version contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

The data on your device stays until you remove it. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the game or by clearing its storage in your device's app settings. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side, and lost progress cannot be restored.

6. Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data about you. Since we do not collect any, these requests have nothing to act on, but you are always welcome to contact us with questions.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features such as advertising or in-app purchases, those services may process data such as an advertising ID or purchase records. We will update this policy before such a version is released and describe exactly what is processed and why. The date at the top shows the latest version.
