Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how Pour Sort ("the game"), made by LowFi Studio ("we", "us"), handles information. In short: your game is saved only on your phone. The only data that leaves it is what the coin shop needs: prices and purchase records. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

1. Information stored on your device

To keep your game going, the game saves data such as your level progress, coins, boosters, owned and selected backgrounds, daily challenge and task progress, settings (sound, vibration, language, colorblind mode) and a list of your recent purchases with their order numbers, only on your device. We cannot see or access it.

2. In-app purchases

You can buy coin packs with real money. Payments are handled by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. To check that each purchase is genuine and to deliver what you paid for, the game uses RevenueCat, a purchase-validation service that acts on our behalf. Each time the game starts (when your device is online), it contacts Google Play and RevenueCat to load the prices and to deliver any purchase that was paid but not yet delivered; when you buy, it contacts them again. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random player ID created by the game (not linked to your name, email or Google account),
  • your purchase records: product, price, currency, time, order number and the Google Play purchase token,
  • basic technical data needed to answer the request: app version, operating system version, device language and country, and the IP address of the connection.

We have switched off RevenueCat's collection of advertising and device identifiers. This data is used only to validate and deliver purchases, to answer your support requests and for our own sales bookkeeping. It is never sold and never used for advertising.

3. Permissions

  • Vibration — a short buzz when you clear a level (can be turned off in Settings).
  • Internet and network state — only to talk to Google Play and RevenueCat (at start-up and when you buy).
  • Google Play billing — to show the Google Play payment sheet.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Play (payments) — Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat (purchase validation) — RevenueCat Privacy Policy

The game contains no analytics, advertising or tracking services. Google Play's billing library may send Google diagnostic data about the payment flow, as described in Google's policy.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

Data on your device stays until you uninstall the game or clear its storage; we hold no copy of it, so lost progress cannot be restored. Purchase records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat for as long as needed for bookkeeping, refunds and legal obligations. To have the purchase records linked to your player ID deleted, email us from Settings › Help in the game (it fills in your player ID) and we will delete them within 30 days.

6. Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 and the game has no features that ask for it. Purchases are made through Google Play, where parents can require approval for every purchase in Google Play's family settings.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export personal data about you, and to object to its processing. Contact us with your player ID (shown in the mail written by Settings › Help) and we will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what is processed — for example by adding advertising — we will update this policy before that version is released. The date at the top shows the latest version.
