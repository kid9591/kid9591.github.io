How we use advertising

We promote our own games with Google Ads app campaigns. We do not run or manage advertising for other companies.

To see how our campaigns perform, we read the reports of our own Google Ads account through the Google Ads API, such as impressions, installs and cost. We only read data through the API; budgets and targeting are changed by our team in Google Ads itself. Only our team uses this data.
