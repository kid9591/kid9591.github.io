Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains how Purroku: Cat Hunt Puzzle ("the game"), made by HDC Studio ("we", "us"), handles information. In short: your game is saved on your phone. Once you win a level online, open your profile or create a transfer code, a copy of your progress is also kept on our own small server under a random player ID, so you can move to another phone, and your leaderboard entry (a name you can choose, an avatar, and your results) is shown to other players. Purchases go through Google Play and need prices and purchase records. There are no accounts with an email or password, no analytics and no advertising. The game is meant for players aged 13 and over.

1. Information stored on your device

To keep your game going, the game saves data such as your level progress, the cats you have found, your points, daily streak and check-ins, hints and other items you own, your best times, settings (sound, music) and the random player ID and secret key described below. We cannot see or access it.

2. Your online profile, leaderboards and cloud save

When your device is online and you win a level, open your profile or create a transfer code, the game asks our server (run by us on Cloudflare) to create a random player ID and a secret key for this installation. There is no sign-in and no email, phone number or real name. Our server then keeps:

  • Your profile: a name, an avatar and a frame. The server gives you a random default name such as "DSC9HR"; you may choose another one. Your name and avatar are shown to other players on the leaderboards. Do not use your real name or any personal information as your name. Names are checked against a list of offensive and impersonating words, and we may change a name that breaks the rules (Terms of Service, section 5).
  • Your results: the number of levels you have completed and the "books" you earned (hearts left in your best run of each level), your fastest time and hearts left for each level, and your result in the daily challenge. These are shown on the overall leaderboard, the daily leaderboard and the per-level leaderboards, next to your name and avatar. Daily results are deleted after one week; the other results stay until you ask us to delete them (section 6).
  • Your cloud save: a copy of your progress so that you can move to a new phone with a transfer code (create the code in the game before you uninstall it); it contains levels, cats found, points, streak, hints and other items you own (including whether you bought "Unlock all chapters"). Sound and music settings stay on each phone. Other players never see it. It is stored on our server without end-to-end encryption.
  • Transfer codes: if you create a code in the game to move to another phone, it is valid for 15 minutes and works once. Our server keeps only a one-way hash of it, until a newer code replaces it or your data is deleted. Never give the code to anyone else: whoever enters it gets your progress.
  • The secret key is stored on our server only as a one-way hash.

The game works fully offline. If the server cannot be reached, the game keeps playing and tries again later.

3. In-app purchases

You can buy hint packs, extra tries, heart refills and "Unlock all chapters" with real money. Payments are handled by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. To check that each purchase is genuine and to deliver what you paid for, the game uses RevenueCat, a purchase-validation service that acts on our behalf. Each time the game starts (when your device is online) it contacts Google Play and RevenueCat to load the prices and to deliver any purchase that was paid but not yet delivered; when you buy, it contacts them again. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random player ID created on your device by the purchase SDK (not linked to your name, email, Google account or your Purroku player ID; it stays the same until you uninstall the game),
  • your purchase records: product, price, currency, time, order number and the Google Play purchase token,
  • basic technical data needed to answer the request: app version, operating system version, device language and country, and the IP address of the connection.

We have switched off RevenueCat's collection of advertising and device identifiers. This data is used only to validate and deliver purchases, to answer your support requests and for our own sales bookkeeping. It is never sold and never used for advertising.

4. Permissions

  • Internet and network state — only to talk to Google Play, RevenueCat and our server (when the game starts, when you buy, when you win a level, open the leaderboard or your profile, or move to another device).
  • Google Play billing — to show the Google Play payment sheet.

5. Third-party services

  • Google Play (payments) — Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat (purchase validation) — RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare (hosts our server) — Cloudflare Privacy Policy. Cloudflare processes the IP address of each connection to deliver it and to limit abuse (for example too many new players from one address), as described in its policy. Our own database and logs do not contain IP addresses, and we have switched off request logging on our server.

The game contains no analytics, advertising or tracking services. Google Play's billing library may send Google diagnostic data about the payment flow, as described in Google's policy.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Data on your device stays until you uninstall the game or clear its storage. Android may also back up the game's data (including your player ID and secret key) to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall; you can turn this off in Android's backup settings. Your online profile, results and cloud save stay on our server until you ask us to delete them: email us (see Delete your data) and we delete everything linked to your player ID within 30 days — profile, results, leaderboard entries, cloud save and transfer codes — and your name becomes free for others. Purchase records are also kept by Google Play and RevenueCat for as long as needed for bookkeeping, refunds and legal obligations; we delete RevenueCat's records of your purchases on request too.

7. Legal basis, transfers and children

We process this data to provide the game and its online features and purchases (performance of the contract) and to keep the game safe and fair (our legitimate interest). Our service providers (Cloudflare, RevenueCat, Google) may process data in countries outside your own, including outside the European Economic Area, under the safeguards described in their policies.

The game is designed for players aged 13 and over (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, if it is higher). It does not ask for an email, age or other contact details, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has used the online features, email us and we will delete the data. Purchases are made through Google Play, where parents can require approval for every purchase in Google Play's family settings.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export personal data about you, and to object to its processing. Contact us and we will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what is processed — for example by adding advertising — we will update this policy before that version is released. The date at the top shows the latest version.
