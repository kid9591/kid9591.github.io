Delete your data

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Purroku: Cat Hunt Puzzle (by HDC Studio) has no accounts with an email or password. Your data lives in three places; here is how to delete each of them. See the Privacy Policy for what each one contains.

1. On your phone

Your progress, cats, items and settings are stored on your device. Uninstall the game, or open Android Settings › Apps › Purroku › Storage › Clear storage, to delete them.

2. Your online profile, leaderboard entries and cloud save

These are linked only to a random player ID and the name you chose. Because names and results are public, a name alone does not prove that a profile is yours, and we will not delete anyone's data on a name alone. We verify it in two steps, using the game itself:

  1. Email us at dev.hdcstudio@gmail.com with your player name exactly as shown on the leaderboard (names are unique, including capital letters and accents). We reply within a few days.
  2. In our reply we ask you to create a transfer code in the game (Cài đặt › Chuyển dữ liệu sang máy khác) and send it back. A code works only once and for 15 minutes, so create it right when you are ready to send it. We use it once, on our server, to confirm that you control that profile, and that use also uses it up.

We then delete everything linked to that player ID within 30 days — profile, results, leaderboard entries, cloud save, device keys and transfer codes — and reply to confirm. Your name then becomes available to others. If the game is no longer installed and you cannot create a code, we cannot confirm that the profile is yours; write to us anyway and we will tell you what else can be done. The data is linked to no email or contact detail, so we cannot find it any other way.

3. Purchase records

Every installation that goes online gets a minimal record at our purchase-validation service (RevenueCat) under a random player ID: app version, country and when it was last seen. It is not linked to your name or Google account, and if you never bought anything we have no way to tell which record is yours; it simply stops being updated once you uninstall the game. If you bought items, your purchases are kept in that record. To have it and the related data RevenueCat holds for that player ID deleted, email us at dev.hdcstudio@gmail.com with the Google Play order number (it starts with "GPA.") from your purchase receipt email, the date and the item you bought, so we can find your records. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm. Google Play's own order records (needed for refunds and tax) are kept by Google as described in its policy.
