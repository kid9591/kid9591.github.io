Delete your data

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Purroku: Cat Hunt Puzzle (by HDC Studio) has no accounts with an email or password. Your data lives in three places; here is how to delete each of them. See the Privacy Policy for what each one contains.

1. On your phone

Your progress, cats, items and settings are stored on your device. Uninstall the game, or open Android Settings › Apps › Purroku › Storage › Clear storage, to delete them.

2. Your online profile, leaderboard entries and cloud save

Delete them yourself in the game: Cài đặt › Xoá dữ liệu trực tuyến › Xoá dữ liệu. This removes your profile, results, leaderboard entries, cloud save, device keys and transfer codes from our server, and your name becomes available to others. Your progress on the phone itself stays, and the online features stay off on that phone until you open your profile, create a transfer code or enter one again. If you share the profile with another phone (through a transfer code or an Android backup), delete it there too, or uninstall the game there first: a phone that still holds the old profile makes a new one the next time it goes online.

If the game is no longer installed

The data is linked only to a random player ID and the name you chose, with no email or contact detail. Because names and results are public, a name alone does not prove that a profile is yours, and we will not delete anyone's data on a name alone, nor on anything shown publicly on the leaderboards (name, avatar, results, or a screenshot of them). The way to prove it is through the game itself: install the game again on the same phone. Android's backup usually restores your player ID, and you can then delete your data in the game as described above. If that does not work, email us at dev.hdcstudio@gmail.com with your player name exactly as shown on the leaderboard (names are unique, including capital letters and accents) and tell us what you tried. We reply within 30 days. We can only delete a profile whose owner we can confirm, so in some cases we will not be able to; we will tell you why.

3. Purchase records

Every installation that goes online gets a minimal record at our purchase-validation service (RevenueCat) under a random player ID: app version, country and when it was last seen. It is not linked to your name or Google account, and if you never bought anything we have no way to tell which record is yours; it simply stops being updated once you uninstall the game. If you bought items, your purchases are kept in that record. To have it and the related data RevenueCat holds for that player ID deleted, email us at dev.hdcstudio@gmail.com with the Google Play order number (it starts with "GPA.") from your purchase receipt email, the date and the item you bought, so we can find your records. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm. Google Play's own order records (needed for refunds and tax) are kept by Google as described in its policy.
