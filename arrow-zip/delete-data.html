Delete your data

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Arrow Zip: Tap Away Puzzle (by HDC Studio) has no accounts of its own. Your data lives in three places; here is how to delete each of them. See the Privacy Policy for what each one contains.

1. On your phone

Your progress, coins, album and settings are stored on your device. Uninstall the game, or open Android Settings › Apps › Arrow Zip › Storage › Clear storage, to delete them.

2. Your Google Play Games cloud copy and leaderboard score

If you signed in to Google Play Games, Google keeps a copy of your progress and, if you opened the leaderboard, your score. You can delete them yourself in your Google Play Games settings (this game's data, or your whole Play Games profile). We do not receive this data, so we cannot delete it for you, but email us if you need help finding the setting.

3. Purchase records

Every installation that goes online gets a minimal record at our purchase-validation service (RevenueCat) under a random player ID: app version, country and when it was last seen. It is not linked to your name or Google account, and if you never bought anything we have no way to tell which record is yours; it simply stops being updated once you uninstall the game. If you bought coin packs, your purchases are kept in that record. To have it and the related data RevenueCat holds for that player ID deleted, email us at dev.hdcstudio@gmail.com with the Google Play order number (it starts with "GPA.") from your purchase receipt email, the date and the pack you bought, so we can find your records. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm. Google Play's own order records (needed for refunds and tax) are kept by Google as described in its policy.
