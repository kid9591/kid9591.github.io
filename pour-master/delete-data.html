Delete your data

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Pour Master (by HDC Studio) has no accounts of its own. Your data lives in three places; here is how to delete each of them. See the Privacy Policy for what each one contains.

1. On your phone

Your progress, coins and settings are stored on your device. Uninstall the game, or open Android Settings › Apps › Pour Master › Storage › Clear storage, to delete them.

2. Your Google Play Games cloud copy and leaderboard score

If you signed in to Google Play Games, Google keeps a copy of your progress and, if you opened the leaderboard, your score. You can delete them yourself in your Google Play Games settings (this game's data, or your whole Play Games profile). We do not receive this data, so we cannot delete it for you, but email us if you need help finding the setting.

3. Purchase records

If you bought coin packs, a record of your purchases is kept by our purchase-validation service (RevenueCat) under a random player ID. To have it and the related data RevenueCat holds for that player ID deleted, email us at dev.hdcstudio@gmail.com, ideally from Settings › Help in the game, which fills in your player ID. If you no longer have the game, include the Google Play order number (it starts with "GPA.") from your purchase receipt email instead. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm. Google Play's own order records (needed for refunds and tax) are kept by Google as described in its policy.

4. Contact
